Program Overview
Two Playbooks. One Operating Engine.

VPS enters through two disciplined modes: Playbook A (equity acquisitions) for assets VPS can own outright, and Playbook B (O&M concession) for mission hospitals that will never be sold. Both apply the same six-lever operating engine proven at Max Healthcare and Aster DM.

Investment Committee Memorandum
The Case for Acting Now.

A ₹3,900 Cr programme to build Kerala's first integrated hospital network across 25 priority sites over 24 months. The window is defined by the Aster–QCIL merger (final NCLT approval) and KKR locking North Kerala — best assets at pre-consolidation multiples are available for 18–24 months only.

Executive Summary
01 — The Window
24 months before the market reprices
Aster is in final NCLT approval for the QCIL merger. KKR has locked North Kerala. Mid-market hospital owners who would never sell to a listed chain will do an O&M deal with VPS Lakeshore. That combination of conditions expires. The programme must be mandated now.
02 — The Moat
Mission hospitals — the asset Aster cannot buy
Playbook B targets Catholic and charitable hospitals that are structurally unsellable. VPS's operational track record, Catholic affinity, and non-listed status make it the only credible operator for these assets. Eight mission / society / turnaround O&M hospitals, zero acquisition premium — fee income only, not consolidated.
03 — Network Economics
Returns are built into the network, not any single deal
Buying six profitable hospitals at ~7× current EBITDA and exiting the network at ~13–14×, the base case now targets ~20% equity IRR and 2.4× MOIC. Three excluded upside vectors — Nettoor referral uplift (₹100–200 Cr est. annual EBITDA to Nettoor), O&M EBITDA-share above threshold, and platform multiple expansion — lift returns toward ~29% IRR / 3.5× MOIC in the bull case.
