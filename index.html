Executive Summary
01 — The Window
24 months before the market reprices
Aster is in final NCLT approval for the QCIL merger. KKR has locked North Kerala. Mid-market hospital owners who would never sell to a listed chain will do an O&M deal with VPS Lakeshore. That combination of conditions expires. The programme must be mandated now.
02 — The Moat
Mission hospitals — the asset Aster cannot buy
Playbook B targets Catholic and charitable hospitals that are structurally unsellable. VPS's operational track record, Catholic affinity, and non-listed status make it the only credible operator for these assets. Four hospitals, ~1,400 beds, zero acquisition premium.
03 — Network Economics
Returns are built into the network, not any single deal
Individual deals may return 20–22% IRR. The combined network — shared procurement saving ₹28 Cr/year, Gulf referral channel adding ₹45 Cr incremental revenue, step-down capacity from Nettoor freeing quaternary beds — targets 28%+ blended IRR and 3.8× MOIC.
