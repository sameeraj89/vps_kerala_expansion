Programme Overview
Two Playbooks. One Operating Engine.

VPS enters through two disciplined modes: Playbook A (equity acquisitions) for assets VPS can own outright, and Playbook B (O&M concession) for mission hospitals that will never be sold. Both apply the same six-lever operating engine proven at Max Healthcare and Aster DM.

Investment Committee Memorandum
The Case for Acting Now.

A ₹3,000 Cr programme to build Kerala's first integrated hospital network across 18 base-case sites (plus 2 optionality) over 24 months. The window is defined by the Aster–QCIL merger (final NCLT approval) and KKR locking North Kerala — best assets at pre-consolidation multiples are available for 18–24 months only.

Executive Summary
01 — The Window
24 months before the market reprices
Aster is in final NCLT approval for the QCIL merger. KKR has locked North Kerala. Mid-market hospital owners who would never sell to a listed chain will do an O&M deal with VPS Lakeshore. That combination of conditions expires. The programme must be mandated now.
02 — The Moat
Mission hospitals — the asset Aster cannot buy
Playbook B targets Catholic and charitable hospitals that are structurally unsellable. VPS's operational track record, Catholic affinity, and non-listed status make it the only credible operator for these assets. Seven base-case mission / society O&M hospitals, zero acquisition premium — fee income only, not consolidated.
03 — Network Economics
Returns are built into the network, not any single deal
The base case is deliberately conservative: ~13% equity IRR and 1.81× MOIC — a strategic-control platform return, priced below a near-term financial-return hurdle. Three excluded upside vectors — Nettoor referral uplift (₹100–200 Cr est. annual EBITDA to Nettoor), O&M EBITDA-share above threshold, and platform multiple expansion — lift returns toward ~23% IRR / 2.83× MOIC in the bull case.
