Programme Overview
Two Playbooks. One Operating Engine.

VPS enters through two disciplined modes: Playbook A (equity acquisitions) for assets VPS can own outright, and Playbook B (O&M concession) for mission hospitals that will never be sold. Both apply the same six-lever operating engine proven at Max Healthcare and Aster DM.

Playbook B · Management Partner
O&M Concession
Entry mechanism: Long-tenor O&M concession (15–25 years). Trust retains land, building, and hospital license. VPS provides management + capital for upgrades.
Best for: Catholic-managed hospitals, religious trusts, community hospitals that will never sell but need operational partners.
Aster DM benchmark: Identical structure proven in Kollam (Kerala) and Karnataka. +25–40% ARPOB improvement in 12–18 months.
