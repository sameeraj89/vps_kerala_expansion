Program Overview
Two Playbooks. One Operating Engine.

VPS enters through two disciplined modes: Playbook A (equity acquisitions) for assets VPS can own outright, and Playbook B (O&M concession) for mission hospitals that will never be sold. Both apply the same six-lever operating engine proven at Max Healthcare and Aster DM.

Tranche 1 · Diligenced & Immediate (June 2026)
The Immediate 11 — Value-Accretive Owned Core

The full 25-target priority programme and the 50-hospital universe remain the strategic build (see the Network Map, Shortlist and Long List tabs below). The plan narrows to a screened immediate tranche of 11 — seven on audited MCA accounts — that VPS can move on first: the owned core plus one O&M turnaround (Aswini), anchored by VPS Lakeshore and the new greenfield Kakkanad PMC. Avitis is dropped (KIMS took it on a 20-year O&M, Apr-2026); Daya is held as a clinical alliance / minority option, not a control buy.

Board ask: mandate advisors and commence confirmatory due diligence on the 3–5 anchor assets — Dr KM Cherian + Welcare, Mother, Meditrina, Ananthapuri. No capital is committed and no execution authority is sought at this stage. Entry-price discipline is the single most important control: re-base the owned-core anchors toward market multiples before any LOI, and hold walk-away discipline on PE-set assets such as Daya.

Investment Committee Memorandum
The Case for Acting Now.

A ₹3,900 Cr program to build Kerala's first integrated hospital network across 25 priority sites over 24 months. Kerala's hospital market is consolidating quickly — listed chains and PE-backed operators are moving on the state's best assets — so the window to enter at pre-consolidation multiples is open for only 18–24 months.

Executive Summary
01 — The Window
24 months before the market reprices
A wave of consolidation is under way and the premium North Kerala tier is being locked up by larger acquirers. Yet mid-market hospital owners who would never sell to a listed chain will still do an O&M deal with VPS Lakeshore. That combination of conditions is temporary — the program must be mandated now.
02 — The Moat
Mission hospitals — the asset listed chains cannot buy
Playbook B targets Catholic and charitable hospitals that are structurally unsellable. VPS's operational track record, Catholic affinity, and non-listed status make it the only credible operator for these assets — a route closed to listed acquirers. Eight mission / society / turnaround O&M hospitals, zero acquisition premium — fee income only, not consolidated.
03 — Network Economics
Returns are built into the network, not any single deal
Buying six profitable hospitals at ~7× current EBITDA and exiting the network at ~13–14×, the base case now targets ~20% equity IRR and 2.4× MOIC. Three excluded upside vectors — Nettoor referral uplift (₹100–200 Cr est. annual EBITDA to Nettoor), O&M EBITDA-share above threshold, and platform multiple expansion — lift returns toward ~29% IRR / 3.5× MOIC in the bull case.
